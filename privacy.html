Privacy notice

Last updated October 16, 2026

Lighthouse is a cybersecurity training program for Maine middle schools, high schools, and school staff, developed by Harper Steenstra in partnership with the Maine Department of Education. This notice explains how Lighthouse handles information.

We do not collect your information

Lighthouse has no accounts and no sign-in. Nothing you type or click, including your answers, reflections, password lab entries, and the name on your certificate, is sent to us or to anyone else.

What is saved on your device

Lighthouse uses your browser's storage, which works like cookies, to remember:

  • Which steps and modules you have finished
  • Reflections you type in the educator course
  • The name, school, and grade or role you enter for your certificate
  • Your cookie choice

This information stays in the browser on the device you used. It is not shared, and you can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. On a shared computer, the next person who uses the same browser can see your progress.

Cookies

The storage above is essential for saving your progress. Lighthouse does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's standard connection information.

Questions

If you have questions about Lighthouse, contact your school's administrator.
